# Tectonic Venue Explorer

Public deployment of the Tectonic Wintercircus and De Vooruit visualization.

The standalone website preserves its sandboxed iframe and Content Security Policy. It includes three Concertzaal event layouts, venue-specific reference drawings, floor controls and street entrance guidance.

This is an orientation model with approximate dimensions. Some upper-floor footprints and proposed event fixture positions are schematic. Source references and qualifications are included in the visualization.

Deployment bundle from the working project's source commit `86a7d69a9a9cb0a7d952f3fc0eaf6c628763c915`.

Vooruit now distinguishes the curved front roof and street-front towers, the courtyard and low glazed bridge, the stage house, halls and rear foyers. Numbered entrances persist across floor/detail filters. Branding and primary controls share a compact desktop toolbar.

The overview places the venues on opposite sides of Lammerstraat, with Vooruit’s front facing Sint-Pietersnieuwstraat. Wintercircus horizontal scale is calibrated against the mapped footprint and roof-plan scale bar. Door labels attach to their portal geometry; the rear Vooruit door is on Parijsberg alley. Mapped streets, sidewalks and Miriam Makebaplein replace the unsupported connector.

Muinkschelde water and banks, Charles Marcellisbrug with its Lammerstraat approaches, and Albertina Sisulubrug now use mapped OpenStreetMap polygons. The river appears in the overview, both venue views and the entrance inset; render elevations remain schematic.
